Business rules — how ranges are stored and matched

A range is keyed by sex + age band (or by age only, depending on the test's Reference type): By Sex and Age vs By Age Only (p. 104).
Granularity rule (p. 104, verbatim): "يجب إدخال نورمال كل وحدة عمر على حدة" — a normal must be entered for each age unit separately. Concretely:
A normal defined for ages 1 → 60 months, then registering a patient aged 15 months or 35 months → the program identifies the normal and displays it in the report.
But a patient aged 1 month is NOT recognised — even though 1 month equals 30 days.
Therefore a new normal must be added for every new age unit (يوم / شهر / سنة — day / month / year).
The range string is unit-bearing (13.5 – 20.5 g/dL) and the unit is a separate column, so a change of unit implies a different range row.
Low / High comments are attached to the range, not to the test — i.e. they are conditional on the patient's sex/age band.
Inputs: sex, age band, lower value, upper value, unit (inherited from the test), low comment (optional), high comment (optional). Outputs: range rows that the report engine resolves; report columns show e.g. Male 11 - 13 sec, Male 75-100 %, Male 1.0-1.2 (p. 103, 116, 117) and inline High / Bad / Normal style flags (p. 102).

Role: lab administrator (screenshots as admin, p. 99, 100). [not explicitly stated]

Dependencies: L3-1/L3-2 (the test and its Reference type), report/result-entry screen (comments surface by double-clicking the comment cell).

L3-3b — Re-evaluating already-registered patients after a normal is changed (تحديث القيم المرجعية)
Pages: 102 (bottom note), 103

Rule (p. 102): "في حالة تعديل نورمال لتحليل معين ووجود مرضى تم تسجيلهم على القيم السابقة — البرنامج يتعامل معهم على أساس القيم السابقة" — if a test's normal is edited while patients already exist on the old values, the program keeps treating those patients against the OLD values.

Override workflow (p. 102):

1.
Open the report (التقرير) of the affected patient.
2.
Click the reference-value cell inside the report → the shown dialog appears.
3.
Press تحديث (Update) → then موافق (OK). → only then are the new reference values applied to that report.
Business rules

Retroactivity is opt-in, per report, and irreversible-by-default — there is no bulk re-evaluation described.
The update is executed from the report screen, not from the master-data screen: the master change alone never rewrites historical reports.
The resulting report can carry both derived values and flags, e.g. for PT (p. 103, 116, 117): rows Control's time, Patient's time, Concentration, INR., Ratio, APTT (Control's time 33 sec), Fibrinogen, FDP, plus a Comment row.

Business rules / automatic behaviour

The entity type determines the downstream behaviour: a doctor is a person-level referrer; a referral/contracting entity is an organisation-level referrer with a bound price list; "sent samples" designates an outside lab.
Price binding is the automation: attaching a price list means order entry no longer requires typing prices — "يتم حساب اسعار التحاليل طبقا لهذه القائمة".
Financial terms are entity-level: discount (خصم) and commission (عمولة) are stored per entity, not per test.
Entities feed patient data, accounts, and statistics — three downstream consumers.
Deletion is confirmation-gated (موافق).
Inputs: entity name, type, contact data, price list, discount/commission. Outputs: a registered counterparty; entity-resolved prices and billing terms. Role: lab administrator / commercial-accounts user. [not explicitly stated]

Dependencies: L3-4 (price lists), L3-1 (base test prices), L3-12 (NATIGH account creation uses this window), L2-1 (the Referred by column in work sheets), L3-6 (per-entity custom groups).

The antibiotic master supports free-text creation of a new drug (p. 138) — not a closed enumerated list.
Deleting an antibiotic removes it from that farm (not necessarily from the master).

2.
Search for the test to be sent outside → select it → press تعديل (Edit).

Business rules / automatic behaviour

Referred-out is a per-test attribute, not a separate test.
The economics of a referral are captured as a margin pair: Cost Price (payout to the outside lab) vs Patient Price (charge) — this is the lab's gross margin on the referral.
The receiving lab is chosen from the external labs registered in the same entities master (L3-7) — so an outside lab must exist as an entity before it can be selected here.
L3-1 item 6 states the same flag is editable from the general test-edit screen ("هل التحليل من العينات المرسلة لمعمل آخر أم لا مع تحديد سعره", p. 95) — L3-10 is the dedicated guided route to the same field, not a different data model.
Role: lab administrator. [not explicitly stated]

Dependencies: L3-1 (same fields, same save), L3-7 (list of external labs), L3-4 (contract price lists for the outside lab).

Business rules / automatic behaviour

Credentials are system-generated, not chosen: the ID button produces the ID; the operator copies it into the password field. The ID doubles as the password.
The account is an attribute of the entity record, provisioned inside the same master as L3-7 — not in a separate user table.
The doctor branch selects entity type طبيب معالج; the lab branch selects جهة إحالة أو تعاقد + the Lab to Lab price list. The two branches differ in entity type, in the price list, and in the patient-registration mode (Contract for labs, p. 147; plain doctor name for doctors, p. 144).
The doctor also carries commercial terms (عمولة / commission %, p. 143) and a contract price list — the portal credential is layered on top of a full commercial entity record.
Visibility is scoped by referral: the doctor sees patients he referred; the lab sees patients whose samples it referred. Access requires the result to have been uploaded to the portal.
Inputs: entity record (type, name, contact, price list, commission), site = NATIGH.COM. Outputs: portal ID + password for the counterparty; result-visibility entitlement. Role: lab administrator. [not explicitly stated]

Dependencies: L3-7 (entity master — this function is executed inside it), L3-4 (the Lab to Lab price list), the results-upload/publishing step (outside this range; the NATIGH portal itself is described on pp. 58–67, outside the assignment).
