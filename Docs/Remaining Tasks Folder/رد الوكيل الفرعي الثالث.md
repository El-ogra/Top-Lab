L1-6 — Patient medical-history data captured at registration
المعلوماتedical تاريخ المريض · Pages: 13, and shown in screenshots p.17, 18, 24, 47, 48, 59, 71, 72

Purpose (p.13): "يمكن إدخال بعض المعلومات عن تاريخ المريض"
Three question groups (p.13, verbatim categories):
1.
هل أخذ علاج ... — is the patient on treatment for: السكر (diabetes) · ضغط الدم (hypertension) · علاج للفيروسات (antiviral) · مضاد حيوي (antibiotic) · علاج ل_siولة الدم (blood-thinning/anticoagulant) · علاج للكبد (liver treatment).

Role: Reception captures; lab/doctor read.
⚠️ Conflict / OCR uncertainty: the period in question 3 is read as خلال يومين ("within two days") on p.13 and p.18, but خلال يوم ("within a day") on p.17 — one of these OCRs is unreliable. Marked uncertain.
⚠️ Conflict: the list on p.13 includes التهاب المفاصل and الفشل الكلوي in the "suffered from" group, while the printed-report form on p.71 asks about imaging "خلال 5 أيام" (within 5 days) — a third, different period. Uncertain / not reconciled by the document.

L1-10 — Compute the patient account
حساب المريض · Pages: 9 (intro bullet), 17 (section 2-1, full procedure), 16 (account field block screenshot), 17 (screenshot), 19 (window screenshot)

Where the total appears (p.17): "يظهر حساب المريض فى خانة (إجمالى التحاليل) ويقوم البرنامج بحسابها طبقاً للأسعار المدخلة فى البرنامج." → Automatic pricing: the program derives the patient total from the prices loaded in the program. Price maintenance is explicitly cross-referenced to chapter 3 (تعديل بيانات وأسعار التحاليل / إضافة قائمة أسعار, p.3) — not covered here.
Account field block (p.16, screenshot): التحاليل · هالى/الباقى · اجمالى · التكلفة بعد الخصم · المدفوع سابقا.
Workflow (p.17):
1.
Enter the amount already paid by the patient into المدفوع سابقا.
2.
Enter the discount amount into the discount field, if any ("وقيمة الخصم إن وجد").
3.
Press Enter on the keyboard twice.
1st Enter → the program performs the accounting/calculation operation.
2nd Enter → saves the operation.
4.
If the patient pays the full amount: press خالص (net / settled) then press موافق (OK) (p.17).
Ad-hoc charge rule (p.17, explicit): "لإضافة مبلغ غير مرتبط بالتحاليل على حساب المريض – نظر خدمة معينة أو تكلفة إضافية – تم اضافتها فى خانة المدفوع عن طريق ادخال المبلغ مسبوقاً بعلامة (+) وتم اضافتها على اجمالى التحاليل." → Prefixing an amount with + and putting it in the المدفوع field adds it to إجمالى التحاليل. This is the mechanism for service fees / extra costs that are not tests.
Outputs: إجمالى التحاليل (grand total), التكلفة بعد الخصم (cost after discount), المدفوع سابقا (paid to date), الباقى / الباقى للمعمل (remaining / remaining owed to the lab).
Role: Reception (cashier). Prices maintained under an admin/accounting role in chapter 3.
Dependencies: L1-4, L1-7/L1-8 (a total only exists once tests exist); L1-11, L1-12.
⚠️ Conflict / ambiguity: p.17 states the + amount "is added to إجمالى التحاليل", but the same page's field block (p.16) shows a separate التكلفة بعد الخصم derived from اجمالى − خصم. The document does not state whether the + amount is added before or after the discount is applied. Unresolved.
L1-11 — Modify / zero the patient account (settlement corrections)
التعديل فى حساب المريض · Pages: 9 (intro bullet), 17 (cross-ref), 18 (procedure + window), 19 (window), 20 (procedure + window)

Trigger (p.18, explicit): "عند الرغبة فى التعديل على المبلغ المدفوع نتيجة ادخال قيمة خاطئة أو تصفيرة الحساب نقوم بالضغط على أيقونة (إجمالى التحاليل) فتظهر لنا النافذة الموضحة، الخاص بالمريض." → Press the إجمالى التحاليل icon to open the per-patient account detail window. Workflow:
Delete a whole transaction (p.20): "يمكن حذف العملية بالكامل عن طريق الضغط عليها ثم نضغط (حذف)." → select the transaction row, press حذف.
Modify an entered amount (p.20): "يمكن التعديل على المبلغ الذى تم ادخاله عن طريق تحديد المبلغ ثم نضغط (تعديل)." → select the amount, press تعديل.

CONFLICTS AND INCONSISTENCIES FOUND IN THE SOURCE
1.
Three different section-numbering schemes. Global TOC (p.3) numbers Chapter 1 as 1-1 … 1-13. The body numbers it 1-1 … 13-1 (p.10, 17, 21, 22, 27, 31, 35, 38, 41, 47, 52, 58, 70). The chapter-local TOC (p.9) uses a third scheme — a bare 1 … 7. The three lists do not line up.
2.
The chapter-local TOC (p.9) omits 6 of the 13 topics documented in the body: التعديل فى حساب المريض (p.18–20), التعديل فى بيانات مريض أو تحاليله (p.22), تقرير فارغ (p.35), إدخال تقارير المزارع (p.38), إظهار جميع زيارات المريض (p.47), متابعة تسجيل الحالات (p.52), and تسليم النتائج عبر NATIGH.COM (p.58). It also reorders items 2 and 3 (search before account).
3.
The chapter-local TOC (p.9) is itself badly OCR-damaged and its intro bullets (lines 10–28) cannot be reliably decoded. The most plausible reading of the three bullets is: (a) how to receive a new patient + print barcode/receipt + show paid & discount; (b) how to enter the Machado/McDonald-type analysis results, allow editing, saving, printing, and how to build that report within the WELLS analysis set; (c) deleting a report by an authorised vs. unauthorised route, moving finished/un-finished results between locations, calculating and settling the patient account, and counting un-finished results. Items (b) and (c) are UNCERTAIN — the test names and verbs are not resolvable from the glyph order. Note (b) references analysis types not covered anywhere else in pp. 9–83.
4.
Section 1-1's actual title is broader than its TOC title (p.10: تشغيل برنامج لاب إب وإضافة مريض جديد vs. p.3/p.9: إضافة مريض جديد) — the app launch + login (L1-1, L1-2) is folded into a section nominally about patient registration.
5.
1-2 is one TOC item but two body behaviours. The TOC (p.3) has 1-2 حساب المريض – التعديل فى حساب المريض as a single entry; the body splits it into 2-1 حساب المريض (p.17) plus an unheaded account-modification block (pp.18–20).
6.
The TOC repeats 1-12 three times (p.3: NATIGH delivery / opening the site / site data on the receipt) while the body has only one numbered header 12-1 (p.58); the other two are unnumbered sub-parts at pp.63 and 69.
7.
الغير محققة vs نتائج لم تراجع — the workspace description (p.12, 23, 25, 27, 35, 42, 49, 58, 80) calls the un-reviewed state الغير محققة; the search narrative (p.42) calls it نتائج لم تراجع. Same state, two labels, never reconciled.
8.
لم تسلم (not delivered) is promised but absent from the filter list. Promised in p.42's narrative and in the window text on 10 pages; no لم تسلم button appears in the p.45/46 filter list, and no procedure exists anywhere in Ch.1 (see L1-39).
9.
Imaging-question period is inconsistent: خلال يومين (p.13, p.18) vs. خلال يوم (p.17) vs. خلال 5 أيام on the printed report (p.71). One of these OCRs is wrong; possibly all three are.
10.
Click-gesture overloading on one screen. p.27: one click selects a patient in the patient list; two clicks opens a test's report. p.13/15/16: two clicks adds or removes a test in the test menu. Three gestures, three lists, one window — high mis-click risk, not mitigated anywhere.
11.
The All (clear-all tests) button is restricted to "عند إضافة المريض أول مرة فقط" (p.13) — there is no documented way to bulk-remove a saved patient's test list short of removing tests one by one (L1-14).
12.
إجمالى التحاليل is both a display field and a button (p.17 display vs. p.18 "اضغط على أيقونة (إجمالى التحاليل)"). Same label, two behaviours.
13.
The + surcharge timing is unspecified — p.17 says a + amount is "added to إجمالى التحاليل", but p.16 shows a separate التكلفة بعد الخصم. Whether the surcharge lands before or after the discount is never stated.
14.
Pricing granularity is inconsistent between the on-screen account and the printed bill. The account (p.16/17) is described as per-test priced; the receipt (p.69) bills per test-group (LIPIDS PROFILE 2 tests 20, LIVER FUNCTIONS 7 tests 65, …). The chapter never explains the mapping.
15.
Lab ID creation sequence is circular as written (p.47): create the Lab ID by "saving after registering data + entering tests", then press (Lab Id) and "save" again. Two saves, ambiguous order. Also p.47 says the program identifies patients for history "by name or by Lab ID" (p.70) while also making Lab ID a precondition for visit history (p.47) — the two statements sit awkwardly together.
16.
History patient-matching is by name (p.70) with no collision/disambiguation rule stated — a real risk in a lab with common Egyptian names, and not addressed.
17.
p.74's settings caption says changes "سيظهر فى هذا التقرير فقط" (appear in this report only) while p.70 presents the same window as the global control for history sorting/grouping — scope of the settings is contradictory or at least unclear.
18.
Section 8-1 is titled "المزارع" (farms) but the workflow (p.38–40) is entirely per-patient Culture/Sensitivity entry, with no explanation of how a farm record binds to a patient. Whether Culture is a patient test or a farm sub-form is unclear.
19.
Unreadable/uncertain tokens deliberately left untranslated (not guessed): مقيد (search button, p.43/45/46/49/81), وده سكا نت ممح (p.44), الكانالنا (column header, p.21), الأجالة (p.24), المهرمخى (p.43), } Hai (p.66), معاد (p.45), لا528 (p.79, Leu), محمد as a truncated user name (p.57), 1R7C 2118 5D3U / 3058112761 (the site's password encoding, p.61/62/65/68/69).
